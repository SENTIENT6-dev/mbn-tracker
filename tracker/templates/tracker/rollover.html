What happens

  • Last year's Q1–Q4 are added to Previous years.
  • Last year's Next year becomes this year's Q1–Q4, split evenly. Adjust the quarters in the MPSR.
  • Last year's Future becomes Next year, and Future starts at $0.
  • The total forecast doesn't change. Each change is logged in the project's history, and the database is backed up first.
